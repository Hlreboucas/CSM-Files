## 2. CURRENT EXECUTIVE STATUS

Banco Inter is in a selective technology-evolution phase.

The bank continues investing in customer-service technology, but not all innovation is being implemented through Genesys capabilities.

The account should therefore not be characterized simply as having low adoption.

The more accurate reading is:

**multi-technology strategy + selective Genesys adoption + concrete investment in Genesys platform/custom development + customer-owned/third-party innovation.**

Current concrete Genesys-related investments include:

- BCP / Org Espelho.
- Custom IPS.
- Agent Qualities Import App through Genesys Professional Services.
- Genesys integration capabilities.
- Audio Connector supporting the customer's automation architecture.
- Recurring CS and executive governance.

At the same time, Banco Inter is evolving its own technology landscape.

Important examples:

- **IBM Watson integrated with Genesys through Audio Connector** for IVR/automation.
- **Banco Inter proprietary Copilot**.
- Other internal technology initiatives that may influence architecture, budget, priorities and future Genesys opportunities.

Some Genesys AI capabilities previously evaluated have not progressed to broader rollout.

Predictive Routing produced a measurable historical POC result but is **not currently prioritized for expansion**.

The Agent Qualities Import App is the most concrete current Genesys PS execution workstream and was **On Track as of 14/09/2026**.

Current account priority:

**execute concrete Genesys platform projects + preserve continuity/BCP + govern multi-technology architecture + preserve proven GPR value for future reprioritization + use strategic governance to identify new Genesys opportunities.**
